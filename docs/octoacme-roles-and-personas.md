# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX and Product Designers translate customer and business needs into intuitive user experiences and usable product decisions. They partner with Product Managers and Developers to ensure that solutions are both valuable and feasible.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, wireframes, and interaction patterns
- Validate design assumptions with user research and usability testing
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature prioritization and user outcomes
- Partner with Developers and QA to ensure designs are implementable and testable
- Contribute to acceptance criteria and edge-case scenarios

### Goals
- Improve usability, clarity, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework by aligning design decisions earlier in the lifecycle
- Support product decisions with evidence from user feedback and testing

### Typical Communication
- Discovery and design review sessions
- Product backlog refinement and acceptance criteria discussions
- UX validation and usability feedback loops

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to shape outcomes and priorities
- Supports Developers by clarifying interaction patterns and edge cases
- Coordinates with QA/Testing to confirm user-facing quality and regression coverage

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, platform decisions, and implementation standards. They help balance product needs, delivery constraints, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, standards, and integration patterns
- Review system design trade-offs and technical dependencies
- Guide implementation sequencing and technical risk mitigation
- Support engineering decisions during planning, estimation, and execution
- Partner with Project Managers to flag delivery and dependency risks early

### Goals
- Improve technical consistency and maintainability
- Reduce architectural debt and avoid avoidable integration issues
- Support predictable delivery while protecting system quality

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency planning and milestone reviews
- Escalation of technical risks to Product and Project Leads

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate product goals into feasible technical scope
- Supports Developers by providing direction, review, and design quality checks
- Keeps Project Managers informed of dependency risks and delivery implications

---

## Security / Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners identify and mitigate data, platform, and compliance risks throughout the delivery lifecycle. They help ensure that projects are secure by design and align with policy requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements during planning and design
- Review technical decisions for risk, compliance, and data handling concerns
- Coordinate security testing, vulnerability review, and remediation follow-up
- Support incident readiness and response planning for product and platform risks
- Help define risk acceptance or mitigation trade-offs with business stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy exposure across the product lifecycle
- Ensure compliance with organizational standards and customer expectations
- Build trust in release quality and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Incident and control discussions during release planning
- Coordination with Developers, PMs, and stakeholders on risk mitigation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers to incorporate secure design and testing practices
- Works with Project Managers to ensure security gates are visible in the plan
- Informs Product Managers and stakeholders of risk trade-offs and mitigation decisions

---

## DevOps / Site Reliability / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
DevOps, Site Reliability, and Operations representatives ensure that applications and services are deployable, observable, resilient, and recoverable in production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Support deployment pipelines, environment readiness, and release automation
- Establish monitoring, alerting, and operational health checks
- Participate in rollback planning and incident response workflows
- Validate scalability, availability, and production readiness requirements
- Work with engineering and PMs to reduce operational risk before release

### Goals
- Improve deployment reliability and operational stability
- Reduce mean time to recovery and production incidents
- Support faster, safer delivery with transparent observability

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Post-deployment validation and incident updates
- Ops planning with engineering and stakeholder groups

### Interaction with existing roles
- Coordinates with Developers on environment readiness and production constraints
- Supports Project Managers in release sequencing and rollback planning
- Provides input to Product Managers on operational impact and release readiness criteria

---

## Customer / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer and Support representatives bring frontline user insight, service experience, and operational context into planning and delivery decisions. They help connect product decisions to customer pain points and support readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, recurring issues, and support trends
- Highlight operational implications for customers and service teams
- Contribute to launch communications, enablement, and support readiness
- Collaborate with Product and Project leads on impact assessment and release planning
- Identify edge cases that may not be obvious from engineering alone

### Goals
- Improve customer outcomes and service quality
- Align delivery with real user pain points and operational realities
- Reduce confusion and support burden during and after release

### Typical Communication
- Product feedback sessions and stakeholder briefings
- Support readiness reviews and release notes discussions
- Customer-impact analysis during prioritization and rollout

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to clarify user needs and impact
- Helps Project Managers identify stakeholder communication needs and rollout risks
- Provides Developers and QA with realistic usage scenarios and support implications

---

## Data / Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics partners help define measurable outcomes, instrumentation, and evidence-driven validation for delivery work. They ensure teams can observe whether the product is delivering value.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and measurement strategy
- Review instrumentation and event tracking for product and operational insights
- Support analysis of usage, quality, and business impact post-release
- Partner with Product Managers and stakeholders on outcome measurement
- Help identify leading indicators and regression signals for decisions

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with evidence and measurable outcomes
- Ensure metrics are aligned with business and customer goals
- Make product impact visible and actionable across the lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Metric definition and dashboard reviews
- Cross-functional insight updates and outcome reporting
- Collaboration with Product Managers and leadership on success tracking

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Product Managers by translating product goals into measurable outcomes
- Provides Developers and QA with quality and usage signals to validate changes
- Helps Project Managers communicate progress and risks through measurable indicators

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers and Delivery Coordinators help orchestrate high-confidence releases and cross-functional readiness. They complement the Project Manager by focusing on release scope, sequencing, and coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, checkpoints, and approvals
- Manage release communications, release notes, and operational readiness reviews
- Track dependencies and minimize release-day surprises
- Support rollback and contingency planning in coordination with operations and support
- Confirm completion of required quality and readiness checks before go-live

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and improve stakeholder confidence
- Increase consistency in rollout practices and communication
- Keep delivery efficient without sacrificing quality and readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness meetings and go/no-go checkpoints
- Coordination with launch stakeholders and support teams
- Updates on dependencies, risk, and operational execution

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers on schedule, ownership, and sequencing
- Coordinates with Developers, QA, and Ops to confirm readiness conditions
- Supports Product Managers and Stakeholders in launch communication and issue response

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles illustrate how cross-functional accountability improves clarity, reduces handoff gaps, and strengthens communication across initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective activities.

## Role interaction model
- Product Managers define the why and prioritize outcome-focused decisions.
- Project Managers coordinate delivery flow, timelines, and communication.
- Developers, QA/Testing, and Technical Leads translate decisions into implementation and validation.
- Security, DevOps, and Data partners reduce operational and risk exposure.
- UX, Customer, and Support representatives ensure that solutions remain user-centered and service-ready.
- Release Managers and Delivery Coordinators connect the work to safe launch and stakeholder confidence.

This broader model ensures that accountability is shared across the full project lifecycle while still preserving clear ownership for decisions, execution, and outcomes.
